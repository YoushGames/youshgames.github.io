Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026 Publisher Yoush Games, Denmark

Defense Time (“the app”) is a free game supported by ads. This page explains what data the app and its service partners collect, why, and how you can control it.

Summary

  • We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, or location.
  • There is no account to create. The app signs you in anonymously so the online leaderboard works.
  • The app shows optional rewarded video ads, which you choose to watch to get extra health or ammo. Our ad partners use your device’s Advertising ID.
  • The app sends anonymous gameplay analytics. You can turn this off and request deletion from the main menu.

Data collected

Device and technical data: device model, operating system and version, language, app version, and your IP address (used, among other things, to estimate your country).

Identifiers: your device’s Advertising ID (used by our ad partners), plus random identifiers created for this app installation and for your anonymous player profile.

Gameplay data: game sessions, the length of a game and your score.

Leaderboard data: your score, your rank and a randomly generated player name. These are visible to other players on the leaderboard.

Ad data: which ads were loaded, shown and clicked.

How the data is used

  • To show rewarded ads and give you the reward. Our ad partners may use the data to measure ads, to prevent fraud and, where permitted, to show ads that are personalised to you.
  • To run the online leaderboard.
  • To understand how the game is played, so we can fix problems and improve it.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for anything other than the purposes above.

Service partners

The app uses the following third-party services, which process data as described in their own policies:

Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and Unity Ads

Ad mediation and video ads. These receive device data, the Advertising ID, the IP address and ad interactions.

Unity Analytics

Gameplay analytics. This receives device data, app identifiers and gameplay events.

Unity Authentication and Unity Leaderboards

Anonymous sign-in and the online leaderboard. These receive a player identifier, your score and the player name.

All of these are provided by Unity Technologies. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and the Unity Privacy Policy. Data may be processed outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in those policies.

Your choices

Analytics: In the main menu, open the privacy panel and turn off the data-collection toggle to stop analytics. Use the delete button to ask Unity to erase the analytics data already collected from your device.

Personalised ads: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings. On most Android phones this is under Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads. Ads may also show a privacy icon that you can use to opt out of personalised ads from that ad network. Watching ads is always optional.

Leaderboard and other data: Email us and we will delete your leaderboard entry and any other data we can link to your player profile.

Local data: Your high score and settings are stored on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the app. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include them in your device backup to your Google account, or copy them to a new phone. This is handled by Google under your own account settings, and we never see it.

Your rights (EU / EEA / UK)

Yoush Games is the data controller. Our legal basis for analytics and leaderboards is our legitimate interest in running and improving the game. You can object at any time by using the choices above. Ad personalisation relies on your consent where the law requires it. You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, and to object to its processing. To use these rights, email us.

Retention

Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboard is active, or until you ask us to delete them. Analytics and ad data are kept by Unity for the periods set out in its policies. We do not keep any other copies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has used the app and you want their data removed, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent to our service partners over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we and our partners take reasonable steps to protect it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same location with a new effective date.
