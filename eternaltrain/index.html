Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026 Publisher Yoush Games, Denmark

Eternal Train (“the app”) is a free game supported by ads and optional purchases. This page explains what data the app, our game server and our partners collect, why, and how you can control it.

Summary

  • We do not ask for your email, real name, contacts, photos or location.
  • You sign in with Google Play Games. Your progress, coins and items are saved on our game server, so they are safe and follow you to a new phone.
  • The leaderboards show your Google Play Games name and your scores to other players.
  • The app shows optional rewarded video ads, which you choose to watch for in-game rewards. Our ad partners use your device’s Advertising ID.
  • You can buy coins and packs with real money. Payment is handled by Google Play; we never see your card or billing details.
  • The app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads where the law requires it, and you can change your choice in the game.

Data collected

Account: your Google Play Games player ID and gamer name, and a player ID that Unity creates for your account.

Game progress: your level, coins, items and settings.

Runs and leaderboards: your score and other results of each run. The server checks each run and purchase, and keeps a note if one is refused. Your best scores appear on the leaderboards with your gamer name, visible to other players.

Purchases: what you bought, when, and the Google Play receipt, so we can give you what you bought and restore it on a new phone.

Device and technical data (collected by our partners): device model, operating system and version, language, app version, and your IP address (used, among other things, to estimate your country).

Identifiers (collected by our ad partners): your device’s Advertising ID and identifiers that the ad SDKs create for this app installation.

Ad data: which ads were requested, loaded, shown, completed and clicked. Our ad partners may also receive your player ID, in-app purchase events and how long the app is used, to measure ads and prevent fraud.

How the data is used

  • To sign you in, save your progress and bring it to any phone where you sign in.
  • To show the leaderboards.
  • To give you what you bought and restore it.
  • To prevent cheating and fraud.
  • To show rewarded ads and give you the reward. Our ad partners may use the data to measure ads, to prevent fraud and, where you allow it, to show ads that are personalised to you.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for anything other than the purposes above.

Service partners

  • Unity Technologies (game server, ads, game engine): Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
  • Google (Google Play Games sign-in, Google Play purchases): Google Privacy Policy

Data may be processed outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in these policies.

Your choices

Personalised ads: The app asks for your consent to personalised ads where the law requires it, and you can change your choice later in the game’s settings. If you decline, you can still watch rewarded ads, but they will not be personalised. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings. On most Android phones this is under Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads. Watching ads is always optional.

Purchases are always optional. You can play the whole game without paying.

Deleting your data: in the game, open Settings, tap Copy next to your Player ID, and email it to us. We will delete your account, progress and leaderboard scores, and confirm by email. We keep a record of purchases only where the law requires it.

Data on your device: the local copy of your progress and settings is removed when you uninstall the app. Your account and progress stay on the server, so they come back when you sign in again. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may also include the local copy in your device backup to your Google account. This is handled by Google under your own account settings, and we never see it.

Your rights (EU / EEA / UK)

Yoush Games is the data controller for the app. We rely on:

  • the contract with you (providing the game you chose to play): your account, saved progress, leaderboards and purchases;
  • our legitimate interests: preventing cheating and fraud, and funding a free game with non-personalised ads;
  • your consent: personalised ads, which you can withdraw at any time in the game’s settings.

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by emailing us your Player ID. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Retention

Your account, progress and runs are kept while you play, until you ask us to delete them, or until we stop running the game. Purchase records are kept as long as needed to restore your purchases and as required by accounting law. Ad data is kept by Unity for the periods set out in its policy. Data on your device stays there until you uninstall the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has used the app and you have questions about their data, contact us.

Security

Data is sent to our server and our partners over encrypted connections, only our server code can change your coins and purchases, and the data saved on your device is stored encrypted. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we and our partners take reasonable steps to protect it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same location with a new effective date.
