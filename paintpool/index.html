Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026 Publisher Yoush Games, Denmark

Paint Pool (“the app”) is designed to respect your privacy completely.

Data we collect

None.

The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or sensitive user data. It contains no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, no account system, and requests no Android permissions.

Data stored on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves a small amount of game data in Android’s private app storage, on your device only:

  • High scores
  • Best combo
  • Games played
  • Coins
  • Unlocked & chosen skins
  • Background
  • Difficulty
  • Sound & music settings
  • Whether you’ve seen the tutorial

We never receive this data. It is deleted from your device when you uninstall the app.

Android backup

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include this game data in your device backup to your Google account, or copy it to a new phone, so your progress can be restored. This is handled by Google under your own Google account settings. We never receive or see it. You can turn it off in your device settings (on most phones under Settings → Google → Backup).

Network

The app does not use the internet and works fully offline.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either (relevant to COPPA and the GDPR’s rules for children).

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same location with a new effective date.
